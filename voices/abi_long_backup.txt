There are two kinds of people who talk to you about a career in financial services. Most people only ever meet one of them. A recruiter sells you the opportunity. A mentor asks what you are looking for. A recruiter shows you the numbers. A mentor shows you the cases. A recruiter is done when you sign. A mentor is still there when you are stuck. Most people get recruited. Almost no one gets mentored. The complaint question is the easy one. A mentor is still there when you are stuck. Most people get recruited. Almost no one gets mentored. The complaint question is the easy one. Everyone asks it. The harder question is the one that actually changes your career. Who is still answering the phone when things get complicated? Because that is the person who shapes your career, not the one who signed you.
